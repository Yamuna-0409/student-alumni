Career Roadmap

Follow a structured path to your dream career with guidance from successful alumni

Return to Home

Foundation Phase

Months 1-6

Build fundamental skills and knowledge in your chosen field. Focus on core concepts, basic tools, and understanding industry standards.

Basic Programming Industry Tools Problem Solving Communication

Skill Development

Months 7-12

Deepen your expertise in specific areas. Work on projects, contribute to open source, and start building a portfolio of your work.

Advanced Concepts Project Management Version Control Testing

Practical Experience

Months 13-18

Gain real-world experience through internships, freelance projects, or personal ventures. Apply your skills in professional settings.

Internships Freelance Work Team Collaboration Client Communication

Specialization

Months 19-24

Choose a specific niche or specialization within your field. Become an expert in particular technologies or methodologies.

Specialized Tools Industry Certifications Advanced Techniques Mentoring Others

Leadership & Growth

Months 25-30

Develop leadership skills, take on more responsibility, and start mentoring others. Focus on career advancement and long-term goals.

Team Leadership Strategic Thinking Mentoring Industry Networking

Expert Level

Months 31+

Become a recognized expert in your field. Contribute to industry discussions, speak at conferences, and help shape the future of your profession.

Thought Leadership Public Speaking Industry Innovation Mentoring Alumni
