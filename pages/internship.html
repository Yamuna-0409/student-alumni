Application Tips from Alumni

Resume & Portfolio

Tailor your resume for each application. Include relevant projects and quantify your achievements. For design/development roles, maintain an updated portfolio.

Cover Letter

Write a compelling cover letter that shows your passion for the company and role. Research the company culture and mention specific aspects that excite you.

Network & Connect

Reach out to alumni at the company before applying. They can provide insights about the role and potentially refer you for the position.

Apply Early

Many internships have rolling applications. Apply as soon as possible to increase your chances of being considered for the position.
